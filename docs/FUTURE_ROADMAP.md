# Future Roadmap

## Current Scope

Six public destinations, six health topics, two complete source-backed articles, reusable article rendering, local search/filtering, an optional email-backed contact endpoint, SEO primitives, accessibility, security basics, tests, and Vercel deployment documentation.

## Possible Future Work

These are architectural possibilities, not approved features:

- Additional medically reviewed articles and categories
- A Git-based, headless, or database-backed CMS
- Editorial author/reviewer workflows and scheduled review reminders
- Multilingual content
- Advanced indexed search after the corpus justifies it
- Privacy-reviewed analytics
- An opt-in newsletter with separate consent and policy work
- Additional author profiles and structured editorial governance

Patient portals, diagnosis tools, records, appointments, payments, and telemedicine remain out of scope unless separately specified and risk-assessed.
