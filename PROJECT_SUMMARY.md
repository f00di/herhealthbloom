# Project Summary

## Project Name

**Her HealthBloom** is the supplied brand name and is centralized in site configuration. The final production domain is not confirmed; `herhealthbloom.com` appears in the source material only as an example.

## Project Owner / Author

Dr. Farkhanda Kashif, FCPS, MRCOG  
Obstetrician and Gynecologist

## Project Purpose

This public website provides clear, patient-friendly, evidence-based educational information about women's health. It is informational only and is not a diagnosis, treatment, emergency, or telemedicine service.

## Main Audience

Members of the public seeking understandable educational information about pregnancy, gynecology, menstrual health, fertility, contraception, menopause, general women's health, breast health, and preventive healthcare.

## Initial Website Scope

The six primary destinations are Home, Articles / Blog, About, FAQ, Contact Us, and Privacy Policy & Medical Disclaimer.

## Initial Health Topics

- Pregnancy
- Menstrual Health
- Fertility
- Menopause
- General Women's Health
- Breast Health

## Initial Content

The requested source articles are:

- *Abdominal Pain During Pregnancy: Causes, Warning Signs, and When to Seek Medical Help*
- *Healthy Eating During Pregnancy: A Guide to the Foods for You and Your Baby*

Both authoritative articles have been imported from the supplied DOCX files as validated structured content. They include the source headings, medical warnings, FAQs, and reference lists. No publication or update date was added because the sources did not supply one. The same content model supports additional articles without a bespoke route.

## Core Product Goals

Trustworthy, professional, evidence-based, patient-friendly, mobile-first, fast, SEO-ready, accessible, maintainable, and expandable.

## Out of Scope

- Diagnosis, treatment, or AI medical diagnosis
- Telemedicine or a symptom checker
- Appointments or prescription services
- Patient records, uploads, or a patient portal
- User accounts
- Ecommerce or payments

## Current Status

**Deployment ready with external launch gates.** Repository implementation and local QA are complete. Public launch still requires the final domain, approved contact configuration, owner/medical/legal review, live email verification, and post-deployment accessibility/SEO checks.
